# MVP Definition: ASENZO

## Core Philosophy
If we could only ship 20% of ASENZO, what 20% creates 80% of the value for our primary ICP (agency founders, consultants, and service-based entrepreneurs)?

The core customer problem is: *"My business grows without me becoming the bottleneck."*

To solve this, the MVP must provide a system of record that shifts the founder from an "operator" to an "owner". It does not need to execute everything for them; it needs to organize the execution.

## The MVP 20% (The "OS Core")

### 1. Foundation (The Source of Truth)
Before any action can be taken, the system must establish the business DNA.
- **Must Have**: Positioning builder, ICP definition, and Voice/Authority ingest.
- **Why**: This is the engine room. Every other module (Content, DM Triage, Sales) must draw from these established facts constraint-bound by the founder's input. Without it, ASENZO is just another generic dashboard.

### 2. Command Center & Founder Independence Signals (FIS)
The central nervous system of the app.
- **Must Have**: The Founder Independence Score (FIS) calculator, high-level metrics across the 5 engines, and the "Active Bottlenecks" alert system.
- **Why**: This is the dopamine center. Founders need to log in and immediately see their FIS score and which exact bottleneck (Attention, Conversion, etc.) needs intervention today.

### 3. Conversion OS (Triage & Pipeline)
The system where demand is captured and processed.
- **Must Have**: The Kanban pipeline for deals, DM inbox triage rules, and basic objection handling playbooks.
- **Why**: For service businesses doing $15k-$50k/mo, the biggest immediate value add is protecting the founder's calendar from unqualified leads and organizing active deals.

### 4. Delivery OS (Client Milestones)
Tracking fulfillment to prevent churn.
- **Must Have**: Basic client milestone tracking and onboarding progress visibility.
- **Why**: The founder needs confidence that delivery is happening without their constant oversight.

## What is Deferred to Post-MVP
- The **Intelligence OS / AI Recommendations Engine**: We will lean on deterministic rules for bottlenecks in the MVP, rather than fully generative LLM intelligence.
- **Complex N8N Automations**: Deep multi-channel integrations (e.g., auto-publishing to X and LinkedIn via complex workflows) defer to Phase 2. The MVP acts as the strategy and tracking layer.
- **Advanced Action Queue**: The MVP will feature a simpler "Next Steps" checklist.
- **Full SOP Generation**: We will rely on predefined template SOPs before trying to generate custom ones dynamically via AI.

## Justification
The existing codebase has built a massive amount of functionality across all 5 engines. The goal of this MVP is to **ship the connective tissue** between Foundation, Conversion, and Command Center so a founder can actually *use* the product. We prioritize stability in the source of truth (Foundation) and pipeline management (Conversion), framed entirely by the FIS score (Command Center).
