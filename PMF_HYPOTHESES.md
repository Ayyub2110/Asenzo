# PMF Hypotheses

This document outlines the core hypotheses establishing Product-Market Fit (PMF) for ASENZO. By explicitly testing these hypotheses, we move from "Let's build an OS" to "Let's prove founders get value from this OS."

## Hypothesis 1: The Commercial Wedge (The Symptom VS The Root Cause)
**Hypothesis:** Service founders will not pay $12.5k for a generic "Operating System" upfront, but they *will* pay to solve their most acute symptom (e.g., unpredictable leads or chaotic DM inbox).
**Test:** The MVP's commercial wedge is the "Foundation + Conversion OS Installation." We sell the resolution of chaotic pipelines and unpredictable sales calls, and sneak the Foundation OS (the root cause fix) in as the requirement to achieve it.
**Success Metric:** Prospects agree to perform the heavy lifting of the Foundation DNA intake because they believe it is required to fix their Conversion pipeline.

## Hypothesis 2: The Founder Independence Score (FIS) Resonance
**Hypothesis:** The concept of a "Founder Independence Score" (FIS) is a more compelling metric for bootstrapped founders than raw lead volume or top-line ARR.
**Test:** Use the FIS score front-and-center on the Command Center. Observe if founders check this score during weekly use.
**Success Metric:** Founders reference their FIS score in discovery calls or feedback sessions, and use the Command Center bottlenecks to prioritize their week.

## Hypothesis 3: The Architecture of Constraint
**Hypothesis:** Founders prefer an opinionated, constrained workflow (ASENZO) over a perfectly customizable empty canvas (Notion / Airtable).
**Test:** We lock down the taxonomy of the deals Pipeline and the inputs for the Business DNA without offering endless custom fields.
**Success Metric:** Less than 20% of beta users complain about missing custom fields; 80%+ successfully transition deals through the opinionated stages.

## Hypothesis 4: AI as a Directive, Not a Chatbot
**Hypothesis:** Founders suffer from decision fatigue and do not want to prompt an AI out of thin air. They want the AI to read their system and issue "Directives".
**Test:** Deploy the Bottleneck alert system in the Command Center instead of a chat icon.
**Success Metric:** High click-through rate (CTR) on system-suggested "Next Moves" derived from AI analysis compared to manual exploration of the OS.

## Hypothesis 5: The "Single Point of Truth" Value
**Hypothesis:** Centralizing Voice, Knowledge, and Positioning in Foundation will noticeably decrease the time required to onboard a new assistant or spin up a new marketing campaign.
**Test:** After implementing Foundation, measure the time it takes a founder to generate 5 pieces of on-brand content compared to their previous manual process.
**Success Metric:** Content generation time drops by 50%+, and the perceived quality and accuracy of the output (assessed by the founder) meets or exceeds manual drafting.
