# Product Non-Goals

To successfully build ASENZO as a Founder-focused Growth Operating System, we must be ruthlessly protective of our scope. Complexity is the greatest risk to this project. 

The following items are **explicitly declared as Non-Goals**. We will not build them.

## 1. We are NOT building an open-ended generic CRM.
ASENZO's Conversion OS is a highly opinionated triage and pipeline system designed for high-ticket service founders. We are not competing with Salesforce or Hubspot. 
- **Non-Goal**: Deep custom field creation, complex custom entity relationships, or exhaustive reporting suites. 
- **Focus**: Opinionated Kanban pipelines optimized for moving a prospect from DM → VSL → Call.

## 2. We are NOT building a native social media scheduler.
The Attention OS is designed to generate highly accurate, business-DNA-aligned content ideas, hooks, and scripts. 
- **Non-Goal**: Direct integration with Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram APIs for the purpose of scheduling posts. 
- **Focus**: Generating the assets and exporting them for external tools (or n8n) to handle delivery.

## 3. We are NOT building a generic AI chatbot.
The Intelligence OS and AI features in ASENZO are constrained.
- **Non-Goal**: An open-ended ChatGPT wrapper where founders can chat with their business.
- **Focus**: Deterministic rules combined with generative AI to spot bottlenecks. The AI should give specific "Directives" (e.g., "VSL Dropoff is high"), not open-ended chat.

## 4. We are NOT building a complete Project Management tool.
The Delivery OS prevents churn by tracking milestones.
- **Non-Goal**: Replacing ClickUp, Asana, or Linear. We will not build complex nested tasks, time tracking, or granular assignee tagging.
- **Focus**: High-level milestone tracking so the founder knows if delivery is healthy without managing the micro-tasks.

## 5. We are NOT reinventing the Frontend Component Wheel.
- **Non-Goal**: Building a complex, custom-coded UI framework from scratch in Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Focus**: Utilizing existing, proven design systems and components to achieve a premium Apple-inspired aesthetic quickly and reliably.

## Summary
When facing a product decision, rely on this mandate:
**ASENZO is the strategic layer that tells the founder what is happening and what to do next. It is out of scope to build the tools that actually execute the work if robust third-party tools already exist.**
