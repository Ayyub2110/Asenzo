# ASENZO — ACQUISITION CENTER

## Master Product Definition + Architecture + Implementation Prompt

The Acquisition Center is the system responsible for turning attention into **measurable, attributable and qualified demand**.
It sits between **Attention** and **Conversion**.
Its purpose is to: **Make the journey from attention → intent → lead → qualification visible, measurable and actionable.**

## 1. ASENZO PRODUCT CONTEXT
ASENZO is a Founder Growth Operating System.
The Acquisition Center manages the journey: Attention → Interest → Intent → Capture → Lead → Qualification → Conversation → Qualified Opportunity
